Radiolack

Radiolack on this computer lets the devices that enter its access code control it and hear it from the local network.

1. Install the certificate

Once per device: the browser then trusts Radiolack's pages without warning. The certificate only vouches for addresses and names of local networks, not for other websites.

Download the certificate

iPhone and iPad
  1. Download the certificate, in Safari, and allow the download.
  2. In Settings, open Profile Downloaded, or General, VPN & Device Management, then the Radiolack profile.
  3. Under More Details, check that its SHA-256 fingerprint is the one that Radiolack's settings show on the computer, then install the profile.
  4. In Settings, General, About, Certificate Trust Settings, turn on full trust for Radiolack.
  5. Come back to this page and reload it.
Android
  1. Download the certificate.
  2. In Settings, search for "CA certificate", choose Install a certificate, CA certificate, then the downloaded file.
  3. In Settings, Trusted credentials, User, check that its SHA-256 fingerprint is the one that Radiolack's settings show on the computer.
  4. Come back to this page and reload it.
Other devices

Import the certificate as an authority that identifies websites, in the browser's or the system's certificate settings, and check its SHA-256 fingerprint against the one that Radiolack's settings show on the computer.
