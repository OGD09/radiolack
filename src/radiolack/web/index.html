          Antenna length

          Wavelength (λ)
          Half-wave dipole
          Each arm
          Quarter-wave vertical
          Each radial

          The dipole and the vertical include the usual 5% shortening: cut them a little longer, then trim.

          Listening needs no resonant antenna: below 30 MHz a long wire often does as well; above, a resonant one mostly helps weak signals.

          Calibrate

          Tune the VFO onto a steady carrier whose frequency you know, in AM or CW: Radiolack measures the strongest line within the filter, which in NFM or SSB may not be the carrier.

          Carrier measured

          Correction in use
          Correction to apply

          A converter is set: its oscillator is taken as exact.

          On the tuner, the result also takes in the tuner's own error at this frequency: for the crystal alone, calibrate in direct sampling.
