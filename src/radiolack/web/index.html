      FrequencyMarginLast seen

      The sweep steps the receiver across the range in overlapping windows, pass after pass, and lists the signals it finds in Peaks and the History. It needs a receiver that tunes, such as an RTL-SDR. It goes on without any interface; tuning by hand stops it, and Stop brings the receiver back.

        The scan watches the ticked bookmarks of the receiver's span at once, and stays on an active one while the squelch is open. With a receiver that tunes, it visits the ticked bookmarks outside the span too, window after window. It goes on without any interface; tuning by hand stops it, and Stop brings the receiver back unless it stays on a channel.

        Radiolack keeps the channels where it found signals for 90 days, apart from the simulated band's: when, how often, how long and how far above the noise they were heard, nothing more. Clearing forgets them for every interface.

        Remote access

        A phone or a tablet on the local network opens the address in its browser, installs Radiolack's certificate and enters the code, once, then gets this interface and the audio. The receiver is shared; each device sets its own volume.
